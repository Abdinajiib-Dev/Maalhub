* **Slide Title:** MaalHub — Connecting Ideas With Opportunities
* **Subtitle:** Democratizing Early-Stage Startup Capital & Direct Investment
* **Presenters:** Abdinajiib Artan Osman & Sumaya Anwar Mumin

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
* **Headline:** The Broken Bridge Between Innovation & Capital
* **Key Bullet Points:**
  - Young founders face high bank collateral requirements and informal gatekeepers.
  - Local & diaspora investors have idle capital but lack a transparent discovery channel.
  - Over 80% of viable university capstone innovations never receive initial funding.
* **Visual:** Split graphic showing an innovator blocked by bank gates vs. an investor searching for credible opportunities.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — MaalHub
* **Headline:** Transparent, Milestone-Driven Venture Funding
* **Key Features:**
  - Direct connection between founders and capital providers.
  - Vetted project showcases across 4 key sectors: Agriculture, Business, Hospitality, and Education.
  - Built-in proposal submission and direct in-app messaging.
* **Visual:** Dashboard screenshot showing active project statistics and proposal inbox.

---

### Slide 4: Key Innovation — Milestone Investment Tranches
* **Headline:** Eliminating Risk with Phased Funding
* **How It Works:**
  - Funding goals are divided into verifiable phases (Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3).
  - Capital commitments are tied to proof-of-work documentation and milestone approvals.
  - Prevents blind lump-sum loss and builds long-term investor trust.
* **Visual:** Diagram of the Tranche workflow with milestone progress indicators.

---

### Slide 5: System Architecture & Technologies
* **Headline:** Modern, Secure, Scalable Engineering
* **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React (Fluid SPA experience).
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express 5, Zod input validation, rate limiting, and security headers.
* **Data & Storage:** Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
* **Visual:** Architecture diagram showing Client -> Express API -> Supabase DB & Storage.

---

### Slide 6: Live Demonstration Highlights
* **Core Flows Demonstrated:**
  1. *Entrepreneur:* Milestone setup, pitch documentation, proposal review.
  2. *Investor:* Sector filtering, project due diligence, direct offer submission.
  3. *Collaboration:* In-app two-way messaging & proposal negotiation.
  4. *Governance:* Super Admin platform moderation.

---

### Slide 7: Value & Practical Impact
* **University Impact:** Commercializes university research and student capstone projects into funded companies.
* **Economic Impact:** Stimulates grassroots job creation and formalized business growth.
* **Investor Confidence:** Brings institutional transparency to local angel investing.
